# A/B replay: legacy DQN vs baseline (browser, no container)

> **ARCHIVED LEGACY DEMO — NOT V2 THESIS EVIDENCE.** This page replays historical
> synthetic CityFlow outputs on an OSM-derived 53-signal topology. It does not show observed
> or calibrated Hai Chau traffic and must not be used for current thesis or defense claims.
> The release-gated v2 path is [`docs/defense/demo-runbook.md`](defense/demo-runbook.md).

Bản 3D (deck.gl, đèn theo từng làn): xem docs/demo_replay_3d.md.

This is the **legacy DQN demo**. It replays recorded CityFlow simulation logs
(`results/haichau_rush/demo/replay_*_demo.txt`) generated on a synthetic 53-node
OSM-derived Hai Chau topology, side by side, frame-synchronized. The scoreboard
reports the actual Chapter 4 cumulative-waiting-time result, computed on the
page by summing the recorded per-step queue series from
`results/haichau_rush/comparison.json` (dt = 10 s) into vehicle-hours: DQN
**−30.8%** and Max-Pressure **−40.1%** waiting-time reduction vs Fixed-Time.
That's a deterministic aggregation of real, already-recorded data — not a
live re-simulation and not an estimate. Nothing on this page is simulated
live — it is a viewer over pre-recorded, real episode logs from the same
scenario/seed (`haichau_rush`).

> **Why replay, not live inference?** An earlier experiment ran the trained
> DQN weights live in the browser on a single hand-built toy intersection. It
> was dropped: the trained policy is tied to the real CityFlow network dynamics
> it learned on and does not transfer to a toy single-intersection micro-model,
> so its *outcomes* there were not representative of the DQN (its real decisions,
> but on out-of-distribution states). This replay demo contains actual logs from the
> legacy simulator protocol, but those historical values are not citable v2 thesis evidence.

## Run the demo
```
python scripts/serve_replay.py --no-browser
# open http://localhost:8080/frontend/replay_ab.html
```
(No CityFlow/Docker container needed at demo time — the replay logs and
roadnet were already generated by the Chapter 4 evaluation run.)

## What it shows
- Two canvases, left and right, each rendering the same `haichau_rush`
  roadnet with live traffic-light phase coloring (green/red dots on each
  controlled approach) and moving vehicles, reconstructed from the recorded
  CityFlow replay text.
- Each side defaults to a different controller (left: Fixed-Time, right: DQN)
  so the improvement is visible at a glance; either side can be switched
  independently via its dropdown among Fixed-Time, RL (DQN), Max-Pressure,
  and Random.
- A scoreboard below the canvases shows each side's cumulative waiting time
  in vehicle-hours for the full episode and its percentage vs Fixed-Time,
  computed from the real per-step queue series in `comparison.json` (the same
  underlying data behind the Chapter 4 numbers) — a deterministic sum, not a
  live re-simulation or an estimate.

## Controls
- **Scenario (kịch bản)** — switch the legacy synthetic demand regime live among `haichau_rush`
  (DQN ▼30.8% vs Fixed in one legacy replay), `danang_peak` (▼13.2%), `haichau_peak`
  (oversaturated, ▼5.3%), and `haichau` (grossly oversaturated, ▼4.8%). Both
  panels + the scoreboard reload to the selected scenario. This makes the
  thesis's honest **saturation story** visible: the DQN's advantage over
  Fixed-Time shrinks as the network saturates, and Max-Pressure stays ahead of
  the DQN throughout.
- **Play / Pause** — animate both panels forward in lockstep.
- **Scrub** — drag to any step; both panels stay synchronized to the same
  simulation step.
- **Speed** — adjust playback speed (steps per second).
- **Zoom cụm nút (cluster zoom)** — toggle from the full-network view to a
  600 m-wide view centred on the **busiest cell of the replay** (`busiestSpot`
  in `replay_ab_core.js`: densest 400 m cell of vehicle positions, sampled every
  10th frame). Use it to see standing queues and individual signal phases.
  *Fixed 2026-07-30:* this used to frame the 8 intersections nearest the
  network's *geometric* centroid — measured empty (0 of 454 vehicles inside the
  view on `haichau_rush`), so the zoom showed bare roads. Vehicle markers were
  also recoloured dark (they were pastel on a light canvas, effectively
  invisible). Vehicles are drawn as fixed ~3 px markers, not to scale: a 2 m
  motorbike would be sub-pixel at any zoom that keeps a junction in frame.
