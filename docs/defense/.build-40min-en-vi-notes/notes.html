Why
A fixed plan repeats all day, while demand does not
G1 32 sG2 28 sG3 22 s3 × 7 s yellowcycle 103 s

An adaptive controller watches traffic and changes the signal decision to cut total queueing. Reinforcement learning learns that rule from the consequences of actions in a simulator.

Why it is hard

Delayed effects. A switch now shortens a queue seconds later and spreads to the next junction.
Conflicting benefits. Releasing one junction can push vehicles into a full downstream link.
Thin evidence. Published cameras cover four movements at one junction on one day.

Idea I take from it

  • Signal timing in Da Nang starts from surveyed flows on signalised arterials, not from a generic city.
  • The network tier drops residential streets (OSM highway.residential) to stay on the main-road corridors.
  • Its method is a coordinated fixed-time plan: cycle, green split and time band set offline from counts.

What I do not take

  • No numbers. Only the title and abstract were read; the full text and survey data were not obtained.
  • The thesis fixed baseline has no offsets, so it is not a green wave.
  • RL here is adaptive: it reacts to observed queues instead of a precomputed band.

Bài toán được xây dựng trên cơ sở bài toán băng thời gian và số liệu khảo sát thực tế dòng xe trên các trục có đèn tín hiệu, kèm phần mềm Green Wave Design. Em kế thừa ý tưởng, không kế thừa số liệu: ở Đà Nẵng, bài toán đèn nằm trên trục chính và bắt đầu từ dòng xe khảo sát. Hai giới hạn em nói thẳng: em mới đọc tên bài và tóm tắt nên không trích số nào; và baseline cố định của em không có offset, nên không phải làn sóng xanh. No label says which action is optimal. The reward is a negative cost, so less negative is better.

Q-value

Return = rt + γ rt+1 + γ² rt+2 + … greedy action = argmaxa Q(o, a)

RL basics
Double DQN and dueling heads fix two different problems

Double DQN changes the target

Plain DQN uses one max to both choose and score the next action, which inflates optimistic errors. Double DQN splits the roles.

# online network chooses a* = argmaxa Qonline(o′, a) # target network scores y = r + (1 − done) · γ · Qtarget(o′, a*)
Worked exampleAction AAction B
Q online−4, chosen−5
Q target−6, used−3
The target network is copied from the online network periodically. van Hasselt, Guez & Silver (2016).

Dueling changes the output head

Q(o, a) = V(o) + A(o, a) − meanb A(o, b)
  • V(o) says how good the state is
  • A(o, a) says how much better each action is
  • Subtracting the mean fixes the V/A split
  • Helpful when many actions are close in value; a design motive, not a guarantee
Wang et al., ICML 2016.
SUMO uses both: Double target, dueling heads, 3-step return.
CityFlow residual uses Double only, with one scalar head.

  • The camera day represents many days or seasons

B9 · Quick answers to common questions
Phụ lục
Backup, for questions
Quick answers to common questions
One model or several?
Two families: SUMO dueling Double DQN (16 → 3 Q); CityFlow residual Double DQN (27 per macro → 1 Q), weights shared across 53 junctions.
Why not a GNN, PPO or a bigger model?
Discrete actions and a small MLP keep the contract checkable. No comparison yet says they would be better or worse.
Is the residual reward the gain over fixed?
No. It is −(queue + pending) over the network in the block. Gains over fixed are measured in paired evaluation.
Loss fell but queueing did not?
Loss measures fit to TD targets on replay; it does not prove the greedy policy is good, so selection uses validation.
Is the system accident-free?
Phase, transition and anchor rules are simulator logic; 0 collisions in the 120 main and 120 sensitivity SUMO runs (the 1,000-realization baseline batch did not record collisions). Not a field safety certificate.
Same γ, different behaviour?
0.99 per 10 s ≠ 0.99 per 300 s. To compare in physical time, raise γ to the number of steps.
A rejected seed tests well?
The validation decision stands; it is reported only as a post hoc diagnostic.
Is this enough for a thesis?
Enough for system development plus simulation evaluation with failure analysis; not for a deployment claim.
