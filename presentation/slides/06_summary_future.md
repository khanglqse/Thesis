# Slide 6: Summary & Future Directions

## Research Summary

### Problem Addressed
- **Urban traffic congestion** causing economic, environmental, social costs
- **Traditional traffic control** unable to adapt to dynamic conditions
- **Need for intelligent, learning-based** traffic signal optimization

### Our Solution
- **Multi-agent reinforcement learning** framework
- **Scalable architecture** for intersection coordination
- **Domain-aware reward design** balancing multiple objectives
- **Practical implementation** with SUMO integration

### Key Achievements
- **48.9% performance improvement** over fixed-time baseline
- **2,483 seconds reduction** in average waiting time per vehicle
- **72.7% improvement** in cumulative reward
- **Demonstrated dramatic superiority** of DQN for traffic control
