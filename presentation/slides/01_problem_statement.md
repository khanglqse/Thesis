# Slide 1: The Real-World Traffic Congestion Crisis

## Urban Traffic Congestion: A Global Crisis

### Economic Impact
- **World Economic Forum**: Urban congestion costs **$1 trillion annually** globally
- **Time Loss**: Average urban driver loses **50-120 hours/year** stuck in traffic
- **Productivity Loss**: Billions in lost economic productivity

### Environmental Impact
- **Carbon Emissions**: Idling vehicles significantly worsen air quality
- **Fuel Waste**: Inefficient traffic flow increases fuel consumption
- **Public Health**: Poor air quality affects respiratory health

### Vietnam Context
- Rapid urbanization in HCMC, Hanoi, Da Nang
- Mixed traffic: cars, motorcycles, buses, trucks
- Infrastructure struggling with demand growth
- Traditional signals cannot adapt to dynamic conditions

## The Core Problem: Traffic Signal Control

Traffic signals coordinate vehicle movement across intersections but current systems fail to adapt to:
- Real-time traffic fluctuations
- Weather conditions and accidents
- Irregular demand patterns
- Mixed vehicle behaviors

**Result**: Inefficient traffic flow, unnecessary delays, cascading congestion
